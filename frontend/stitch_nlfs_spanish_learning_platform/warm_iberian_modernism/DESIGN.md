## Brand & Style

This design system channels the warmth, energy, and rich architectural light of Spain into a structured, highly functional educational interface. It strikes a balance between contemporary SaaS utility and Mediterranean cultural vibrancy. 

### Core Personality
- **Warm & Encouraging:** Welcoming to language learners of all proficiency levels, replacing the clinical stiffness of typical e-learning tools with sunny, inviting tones and friendly geometry.
- **Modern & Rigorous:** Balanced by clean layouts, generous whitespace, structured data modules, and clear hierarchy, ensuring cognitive load remains low during intense study sessions.
- **Culturally Grounded:** Reflects earthy terracotta tiles, vibrant Iberian flora, and deep coastal seas without succumbing to cliché or pastiche.

### Aesthetic Direction
The visual style is **Contemporary Tactile Minimalism**. Surfaces feel substantial, inviting touch and interaction through subtle ambient warm shadows, rounded geometry (12px to 16px radii), and high-legibility typographic hierarchies. Gamification modules—streaks, mastery bars, and vocabulary decks—are integrated as crisp, structured artifacts rather than noisy arcade widgets.

## Colors

The palette balances earthy warmth with high-contrast functional legibility:

- **Primary (`#C85A32` - Terracotta Clay):** Represents foundational progress, major action buttons, active navigation markers, and key brand moments. Communicates artisanal warmth and craftsmanship.
- **Secondary (`#E24A4A` - Sangria Coral):** Drives high-energy engagement points, streak counters, critical prompts, interactive alerts, and celebratory achievement micro-interactions.
- **Tertiary (`#D99B26` - Warm Ochre / Sun Gold):** Reserved for XP counters, badges, mastery milestones, star ratings, and celebratory accents.
- **Neutral (`#1E293B` - Deep Mediterranean Slate):** Anchors high-density text, data display, and dark navigational rails, providing deep, fatigue-free contrast against lighter backgrounds.

### Surface System
- **Canvas Base:** Soft warm cream/sand (`#FAF7F2`) prevents eye strain during extended reading and practice sessions.
- **Surface Elevation:** Crisp white (`#FFFFFF`) surfaces float above the canvas for modules, flashcards, vocabulary drills, and modular cards.
- **Muted Borders & Dividers:** Warm sand outlines (`#EFE8DE`) keep card borders tactile and grounded without visual harshness.

## Typography

The design system exclusively adopts **Plus Jakarta Sans** across headlines, interface labels, and continuous body text. Its geometric underpinnings paired with humanist terminal details evoke modern clarity while maintaining friendliness.

- **Pronunciation & Accent Legibility:** High x-height ensures Spanish diacritical marks (tildes, acute accents, umlauts like *ñ*, *á*, *ü*) and inverted punctuation (*¿*, *¡*) render with exceptional optical balance and clarity.
- **Weights & Hierarchy:** Font weights are constrained to 400 (Body text), 600 (Section headings, interactive states), and 700 (Major module titles and quantitative stats).
- **Tabular Numerics:** For time counters, streak stats, and grammar completion ratios, enable `font-feature-settings: "tnum"` to prevent baseline jiggle during real-time updates.

## Layout & Spacing

The interface uses a 12-column responsive fluid grid on desktop, scaling to an 8-column layout on tablets and a 4-column layout on mobile devices.

### Structural Shell
- **Desktop (>= 1024px):** A persistent 280px left navigation rail (styled in Mediterranean Slate or Cream Base depending on view hierarchy) sits beside a main canvas restricted to a maximum content width of 1280px.
- **Tablet (768px - 1023px):** The rail collapses to a slim icon-only sidebar (72px), while grid margins reduce to `1.5rem` to prioritize learning workspace width.
- **Mobile (< 768px):** Navigation transitions to a bottom bar or overlay sheet. Margins compress to `1rem` to ensure maximum viewport utility for cards and exercise interactions.

### Rhythmic Philosophy
All vertical and horizontal spacing aligns with an 8pt base grid (`space-xs` = 4px, `space-sm` = 8px, `space-md` = 16px, `space-lg` = 24px, `space-xl` = 40px). Interactive practice items (e.g., matching tiles, multiple-choice options) maintain strict `space-md` gaps to optimize tap and click precision.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual depth is achieved through ambient, warm-tinted shadows layered over soft cream surfaces, avoiding cold gray drop-shadows.

### Shadow Characteristics
- **Level 1 (Card Resting / Tile):** `0 2px 8px -2px rgba(44, 30, 22, 0.05), 0 1px 4px -1px rgba(44, 30, 22, 0.03)` with a subtle `1px solid #EFE8DE` border.
- **Level 2 (Hover / Active Interactive Card):** `0 8px 20px -4px rgba(200, 90, 50, 0.12), 0 4px 8px -2px rgba(44, 30, 22, 0.04)`.
- **Level 3 (Modal / Floating Lesson Drawer):** `0 20px 32px -8px rgba(30, 41, 59, 0.16), 0 8px 16px -4px rgba(30, 41, 59, 0.08)`.

### Tonal Hierarchy
- Base canvas remains unshadowed at `#FAF7F2`.
- Content cards use `#FFFFFF` fill with Level 1 elevation to stand out cleanly from the sand background.
- Gamified floating badges and active drag-and-drop word chips trigger Level 2 with a warm terracotta aura to visually signal active manipulation.

## Shapes

The design system adopts a balanced **Rounded (Level 2)** geometry to reinforce approachable learning without sacrificing utility.

- **Base Radius (`0.5rem` / 8px):** Checkboxes, input field interiors, small interactive chips, and inline status badges.
- **Card & Container Radius (`1rem` / 16px):** Primary lesson cards, vocabulary flipboards, grammar review containers, and sidebar modules.
- **Interactive Exercise Tiles (`0.75rem` / 12px):** Sentence builder tokens, multiple-choice option buttons, and audio pronunciation triggers.
- **Pill Radius (`9999px`):** Gamification status badges, level selectors, user streak counters, and primary call-to-action buttons.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary:** Solid `#C85A32` fill, `#FFFFFF` text, `rounded-full` (pill) or `rounded-lg` (12px). Features a 2px offset border effect or bottom border accent (`#A24422`) to provide a tactile push effect when clicked.
- **Secondary / Ghost:** Transparent background with a 1.5px `#C85A32` or `#EFE8DE` border and `#C85A32` text. Hover transitions to `rgba(200, 90, 50, 0.08)`.
- **Accent (Gamified Actions):** `#E24A4A` (Sangria) background for streak actions, speed trials, or test submissions.

### Gamification & Progress Badges
- **Pill Badges:** Fully rounded (`rounded-full`) tags displaying current CEFR level (e.g., "A1 - Principiante", "B2 - Intermedio"), daily XP, or streak numbers. Uses light tints of the key colors (e.g., Terracotta 10% fill with full primary text).
- **Progress Trackers:** 8px to 12px tall continuous rounded bars with a muted cream track (`#EFE8DE`) and an animated fill in `#C85A32` or `#D99B26`, complete with a soft glossy highlight at the leading edge.

### Flashcards & Practice Modules
- Resting on a `#FFFFFF` container with `rounded-xl` (16px) corners and a 1px `#EFE8DE` border.
- Hover states elevate the card with warm terracotta ambient shadowing.
- Flashcard flip states maintain smooth 3D perspective transforms with a subtle warm ochre rim on back-side notes and grammar insights.

### Inputs & Selection Tiles
- **Text Inputs:** `#FFFFFF` background, 1.5px border in `#EFE8DE`, transitioning to a 2px `#C85A32` outline on focus with a matching 3px diffuse focus ring (`rgba(200, 90, 50, 0.15)`).
- **Interactive Sentence Tiles:** Selectable Spanish word tokens featuring Level 1 elevation, `12px` rounded corners, and crisp bold typography. Selected state turns the tile border into solid `#C85A32` with an ultra-light terracotta background (`#FDF5F2`).
- **Checkboxes & Radios:** Rounded-md (6px) for checkboxes, circular for radios, utilizing `#C85A32` active states and a subtle tactile click animation.
